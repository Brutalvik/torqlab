# Terms implementation and legal review

Draft created October 21, 2026. Not a guarantee of compliance or enforceability. Obtain Alberta counsel review before using it as a customer contract. The contact fields remain blank at the owner's request; do not request acceptance of an incomplete actual work order.

Review against TorqLab's actual AMVIC licensing, mobile service/direct-sales process, written work order, cancellation notices, warranty policy, taxes, fees, insurance and privacy practices. No licence status or warranty duration was supplied or asserted. The frontend does not collect legally operative assent or signatures.

Customer handover acknowledgement preserves hidden-defect and workmanship rights. Customer-supplied product exclusions are tied to product-caused losses and do not exclude negligent installation or mandatory liability. Additional charges require prior customer authorization. No statutory cancellation rights are waived.
